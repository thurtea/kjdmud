# Website redesign notes

Extracted from a scratch folder (kjdmud-2) before it was deleted on 2026-09-20.
These are ideas, not a spec. Nothing here is committed to.

## Reference site

https://ldmud.eu was noted as a style reference for another LPC driver's
project site. Worth looking at for layout ideas only. LDMud's own page
content and license text belong to that project, not kjdmud, and should
not be copied.

## Logo idea

An emblem with three snakes, a cogwheel behind the emblem, and a sword
in front of both. No further detail was recorded (colors, style, or who
would produce it).

## Navigation idea

The reference site groups pages as Overview, History, Development,
Documentation, Download, and Additional Resources. kjdmud's current
website/ already covers Overview, Download, Documentation, and the web
client. A History and a Development (or Roadmap-facing) page are the
two categories kjdmud does not yet have, if this direction is picked up
later.
